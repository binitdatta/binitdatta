HOW I APPROACH ARCHITECTURE

Architecture is a bridge between business consequence and engineering detail.

These principles summarize the through-line from early ERP work to cloud-native identity and modern AI systems.

Start with the operating model

Understand the business process, users, money movement, controls, risk and organizational boundaries before choosing technology.

Design the whole system

Application, data, integration, identity, security, observability, cost and operations belong in the same architecture conversation.

Stay close to implementation

An architecture is credible when it survives code, configuration, integration failures, security constraints and production realities.

Communicate across levels

Translate C-suite priorities into product choices, delivery plans and engineering decisions without losing intent between layers.

Design for change

Favor modularity, extension points, standards and clear contracts so systems can evolve as business requirements change.

Engineer for consequences

For enterprise systems, failure modes, auditability, compliance, availability, security and cost are architecture—not footnotes.
